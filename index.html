A seasonal guide for southern Alberta homes

I inspected houses around Calgary until I retired. Weather here does specific things to a house, and most of the damage I found was slow and preventable. This guide goes season by season, system by system.

Checking for Ice Dams on Your Roof During a Chinook

That warm winter wind is a welcome break, but it's also a stress test for your roof. An ice dam is a sign of heat loss from your attic, and a chinook can make a small problem much worse.
